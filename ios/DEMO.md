# The 90-second phone demo

The app demo works because it isn't a mockup with lorem ipsum — it's a
real avatar with a real life in it. Run this order; it builds.

**Before they arrive:** be on the LAN with the node, open **Settings →
Sync from my node**, and confirm it says *"merged N memories."* The phone
now holds 200+ real memories: interviews, journal-mined moments, what the
eye saw, what the ear heard, and the avatar's own nightly conclusions.
Also confirm Venice has Diem, and put the phone on Do Not Disturb.

---

### 1. Open on the flame (5s)
Let the home screen breathe for a beat before you say anything. Then tap
the header → **Avatar Home**: the flame at full size, the mood, and the
count of a life — *203 memories · 9,481 words · N-day vigil* — with the
kinds beneath it: interviews, journal, observation, reflection, ambient.

> "This is a real person's archive. Mine. It's been collecting for weeks."

### 2. Ask it something only it would know (20s)
**Talk** → ask about something specific in your corpus — a person, a
place, a habit. It answers in your voice, from your memories, **out loud**.

> "It's not a chatbot with a personality prompt. It's answering from a
> corpus of my life that I own as a file."

### 3. Let it interview you (20s)
**Ask me** → it asks a question it banked last night at twilight, unasked
by anyone. Answer **by voice**. Watch the memory commit — haptic, streak.

> "It wondered that on its own, while I slept. That's the daily loop:
> one question, one memory, for years."

### 4. Show it something (15s)
**Write** → *Open the eye* → point at the room. It describes what it sees
in the first person and keeps the words.

> "The photo is already gone. It keeps impressions, never images —
> that's the privacy architecture, not a setting."

### 5. The corpus is the product (15s)
**Corpus** → search a word ("dad", "coffee") → tap a memory to expand →
long-press → **Share as card** → then the **Export** button.

> "One open file. If we disappear tomorrow, nothing here dies. That's why
> a family will commit to this for decades."

### 6. Close on the network (15s)
**Settings** → show *Sync from my node* and Twilight.

> "The phone is one body. The same corpus runs on a device in my house,
> reflects nightly on its own, and posts to a public network. The app
> collects the life; the network keeps it alive after."
